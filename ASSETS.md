# Obrazy demonstracyjne

Wszystkie obrazy w `assets/` powstały przez wbudowane imagegen jako ilustracje fikcyjnego miejsca. Nie przedstawiają prawdziwych zdarzeń, map satelitarnych ani transmisji z dronów. Prompty zapisano także w metadanych plików PNG.

## `sector-satellite.png`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: local background image for an interactive 2D satellite-style operational map in a fictional emergency demo app. Produce a high-resolution wide overhead orthographic aerial satellite photograph of a believable small Central European town edge after a storm: dense dark green forest along the west, patchwork farm fields, a modest cluster of houses and industrial sheds toward northeast, pale grey winding roads with one road bridge, winding shallow river and wet floodplain cutting diagonally through center, mixed tree canopy and realistic roofs, natural late morning light. Map-like aerial top-down perspective from very high altitude, no camera tilt, no UI, no grid, no marker symbols, no labels, no text, no watermark, no logos, no border. Clear enough that road and river corridors remain readable under overlaid map UI. This is a fictional location, do not reconstruct a real place.

## `drone-road.png`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide still frame for simulated drone camera feed in a local rescue operations demo. A convincing drone-camera view from 85 meters altitude at a 35 degree oblique angle across a storm damaged rural road in Central Europe, a large fallen tree blocking the road near a small bridge, shallow floodwater around the verge, dense forest edge and scattered houses in the distance. Natural overcast daylight, realistic terrain detail, high resolution, wide landscape composition. No people identifiable, no visible drone, no built-in camera UI, no labels, no text, no watermark, no logos. This is fictional illustrative imagery, not real incident footage.
